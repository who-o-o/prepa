\normalsize \underline{Wild animals' captivity under scrutiny}\\

In recent years, a rise in concern toward animal wellbeing lead 
to opposition against the captivity of wild species. The first 
article (doc 1) published in \underline{Born free USA}, blames
circuses while in the 2nd artcile (doc2), on the website \underline{theguardian.com}, 
on February 2020, journalist Robin Mc Kie targets zoos. Published on the 
\underline{RFI} on January 2021, the third document (doc3) reports the end of Parc 
Astérix's dolphin shows. The last document (doc4) is a promotional image 
featuring captive wild animals. These documents beg the question : is animal captivity 
an enemy to wildlife ? While it can be exploitative it can sometimes be necessary for conservation\\

First, the captivity of wild animals can certainly be harmful for their living 
conditions. Doc 1 demonstrates just how horrible for them the circus life can get, as 
people exploit them for a profit, without caring about education or the protection 
of these endangered species. Moreover, even in more hospitable places,
like zoos, those animals cannot show their true nature in those 
small enclosed spaces, as underlines doc2. And this is not an opinion 
reserved to fierce activists, as in the general French population, 
doc 4 reminds that 70\% of people think "animal rights"
aren't taken "seriously enough" by politicians. However, circuses keep 
selling this glamourous image of happy animals on doc 4, smiling anthropomorphised 
animals, completelt devoid of their original nature, to better appeal to the public. \\

However, it can't be ignored that keeping wildlife in captivity 
is sometimes unavoidable in order to preserve planet 
Earth. Sometimes, endangered species are present 
in captivity because they'd go extinct otherwise. If they weren't 
put in captivity temporarily, as doc 2 reminds us. Another 
use for zoos can be for studying species to better 
understand and protect them. Doc 1 even reminds us that 
it is in fact, the very first function of zoos historically.
